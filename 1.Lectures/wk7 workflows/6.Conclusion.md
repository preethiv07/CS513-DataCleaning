# Lecture: Workflow Automation and Provenance – Conclusion

## ✅ Summary

This brief conclusion wraps up the week’s lectures on **workflow automation** and **provenance**. It reinforces the key idea that workflows are about **ASAP**:

- **A** – Automation: Reduce manual steps through repeatable workflows.
- **S** – Scaling: Handle large datasets and complex tasks.
- **A** – Abstraction: Create reusable, modular pipelines.
- **P** – Provenance: Capture the processing history and data lineage.

The next lectures will focus on **provenance** in detail, emphasizing its role in tracing the origin, transformations, and usage of data in scientific workflows.
